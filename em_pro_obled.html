Emotional Profile Visualization.

This emotional profile visualization was created to showcase dominant emotions, based on a methodology combining sentiment analysis and emotion recognition. In the first stage, the list of tags was analyzed, where each emotion was assigned an appropriate category based on context and associations. Then, using Pandas libraries in Python, the occurrences of individual emotions were counted to identify those that appear most frequently. Based on the results obtained, emotions were presented visually on the HTML page, where each of them received a unique background color, icon, and percentage share, thereby facilitating understanding of the emotional character of the work.
